# Backlink Outreach Plan — Obiomacare.com

**Objective:** Acquire 20–30 quality backlinks from nursing schools, educator blogs, and nursing resource sites within 90 days.

**Strategy:** Value-first outreach — offer free resources, guest posts, or collaboration in exchange for a link.

---

## Target Categories

### Tier 1: Nursing Schools (EDU links — highest authority)
- Nursing school resource pages
- Student success centers
- Career services pages
- Library resource guides

### Tier 2: Nursing Educators & Influencers
- Nurse educator blogs
- Nursing YouTubers with websites
- Instagram nursing educators
- Nursing podcast hosts

### Tier 3: Nursing Resource Sites
- NCLEX prep blogs (non-competing)
- Nursing student communities
- Professional nursing organizations
- Nurse residency programs

## Tools Needed

- **Email finder:** Hunter.io, Snov.io, or Apollo.io
- **Email tracking:** Mailtrack or Streak
- **CRM:** Simple Google Sheet or Notion database
- **Domain authority checker:** Ahrefs, Moz, or SEMrush

---

## Notes

- **Personalize every email.** Mention a specific post, resource, or achievement.
- **Follow up once** after 7–10 days if no response.
- **Don't mass-email.** Nursing is a small community; reputation matters.
- **Offer value first.** Links come naturally when you're genuinely helpful.
- **Track everything.** Know what works and what doesn't.
