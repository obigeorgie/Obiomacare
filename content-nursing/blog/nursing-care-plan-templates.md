## The 5-Column Care Plan Template

Use this structure for every care plan. It matches what most nursing schools require and what the NCLEX expects you to understand.

| Column | What to Include | Example |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **1. Nursing Diagnosis** | NANDA label + related to + as evidenced by | Ineffective Airway Clearance related to excessive secretions as evidenced by crackles, O₂ sat 88%, productive cough |
| **2. Assessment Data** | Subjective + objective findings | Subj: "I can't catch my breath." Obj: RR 28, crackles bilateral bases, O₂ sat 88% on RA, thick yellow sputum |
| **3. Goals/Outcomes** | SMART goals with timeline | Patient will maintain O₂ saturation ≥ 92% on 2L NC within 4 hours |
| **4. Nursing Interventions** | Evidence-based actions with rationale | 1. Elevate HOB 30–45° (improves lung expansion) 2. Encourage cough/deep breathe q2h (mobilizes secretions) 3. Administer albuterol nebulizer as ordered (bronchodilation) |
| **5. Evaluation** | Did the patient meet the goal? | 8/11 1400: O₂ sat 94% on 2L NC. RR 20. Lung sounds clear bilaterally. Goal met. |

### How to Write a Nursing Diagnosis (PES Format)

```
[Problem] + related to [Etiology] + as evidenced by [Signs/Symptoms]
```

**Problem:** NANDA-approved diagnosis (what the patient is experiencing)
**Etiology:** The cause or contributing factor (related to)
**Signs/Symptoms:** Evidence that the problem exists (as evidenced by)

**Examples:**
- ❌ Incorrect: "Pneumonia" (this is a medical diagnosis)
- ✅ Correct: "Ineffective Airway Clearance related to tracheobronchial infection as evidenced by productive cough, crackles, and O₂ sat 88%"

## Sample Care Plans by Category

### 1. Respiratory: Pneumonia

| Nursing Diagnosis | Ineffective Airway Clearance related to tracheobronchial infection as evidenced by productive cough, crackles bilateral bases, O₂ sat 88% on room air |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "I can't catch my breath." Obj: Temp 38.6°C, RR 28, crackles bilateral bases, thick yellow sputum, O₂ sat 88% RA |
| **Goal** | Patient will maintain O₂ saturation ≥ 92% on supplemental O₂ within 4 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. Elevate HOB 30–45° 2. Encourage cough/deep breathing q2h 3. Administer prescribed antibiotics and bronchodilators 4. Maintain hydration (PO/IV) 5. Monitor O₂ sat q4h |
| **Evaluation** | 8/11 1400: O₂ sat 94% on 2L NC. RR 20. Lung sounds improved. Goal met. |

### 2. Cardiovascular: Heart Failure

| Nursing Diagnosis | Fluid Volume Excess related to compromised regulatory mechanism (decreased cardiac output) as evidenced by JVD, peripheral edema 3+, crackles, weight gain 3 lbs in 2 days |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "My legs are so swollen, and I can't breathe when I lie down." Obj: BP 168/94, HR 110, RR 24, JVD +3, bilateral crackles, 3+ pitting edema lower extremities, weight 178 lbs (baseline 175) |
| **Goal** | Patient will exhibit decreased fluid overload as evidenced by JVD ≤ +1 and absence of crackles within 48 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. Daily weights same time/same scale 2. Strict I&O 3. Sodium-restricted diet (< 2g/day) 4. Administer diuretics as ordered (furosemide) 5. Elevate legs when sitting 6. Monitor electrolytes (K⁺, Na⁺) |
| **Evaluation** | 8/13 0800: Weight 175.5 lbs. JVD +1. Crackles decreased to bases only. Edema 1+ bilateral. Goal partially met — continue plan. |

### 3. Neurological: Stroke (CVA)

| Nursing Diagnosis | Risk for Aspiration related to impaired swallowing (dysphagia) as evidenced by coughing with oral intake, slurred speech, left-sided facial droop |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: Difficulty swallowing pills. Obj: Left facial droop, dysarthria, coughing after drinking water, gag reflex diminished on left, NIHSS score 8 |
| **Goal** | Patient will remain free of aspiration episodes throughout hospitalization |
| **Interventions** | 1. NPO until swallow evaluation completed 2. Keep suction at bedside 3. Position upright ≥ 90° for all oral intake once cleared 4. Provide thickened liquids per SLP recommendations 5. Monitor lung sounds q4h 6. Oral care q2h |
| **Evaluation** | 8/11 1600: Swallow study completed — cleared for nectar-thick liquids and pureed solids. No coughing with meals. No signs of aspiration. Goal met. |

### 4. GI: Post-Op Abdominal Surgery

| Nursing Diagnosis | Acute Pain related to surgical incision and tissue trauma as evidenced by grimacing, guarding abdomen, pain rating 8/10, reluctance to move |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "It hurts too much to move." Obj: Pain 8/10, guarding incision site, reluctant to ambulate, incision clean/dry/intact, no signs of infection |
| **Goal** | Patient will report pain ≤ 3/10 within 30 minutes of analgesic administration |
| **Interventions** | 1. Administer prescribed analgesics on schedule (don't wait for severe pain) 2. Teach splinting technique for coughing/moving 3. Reposition q2h 4. Offer nonpharmacologic measures (relaxation, distraction) 5. Assess pain q4h and prn |
| **Evaluation** | 8/11 1430: Patient reports pain 2/10 after morphine 4 mg IV. Participated in ambulation to hallway. Goal met. |

### 5. Renal: Acute Kidney Injury

| Nursing Diagnosis | Risk for Imbalanced Fluid Volume related to compromised regulatory mechanism (decreased GFR) as evidenced by oliguria (UOP 15 mL/hr), BUN 68, Cr 3.2, hyperkalemia 5.8 |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "I'm not urinating much." Obj: UOP 360 mL/24hr (15 mL/hr), BUN 68, Cr 3.2, K⁺ 5.8, edema 2+ bilateral, BP 162/88, lung crackles |
| **Goal** | Patient will maintain urine output ≥ 30 mL/hr and stable electrolytes within 72 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. Strict I&O 2. Daily weights 3. Monitor electrolytes q6h (especially K⁺) 4. Fluid restriction per orders 5. Prepare for possible dialysis 6. Cardiac monitoring (watch for peaked T waves) |
| **Evaluation** | 8/14 0600: UOP 45 mL/hr. K⁺ 4.8. BUN 52, Cr 2.8. Edema 1+. Goal met — continue monitoring. |

### 6. Endocrine: Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)

| Nursing Diagnosis | Deficient Fluid Volume related to osmotic diuresis (hyperglycemia) as evidenced by polyuria, polydipsia, dry mucous membranes, poor skin turgor, Kussmaul respirations |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "I'm so thirsty and I've been urinating constantly." Obj: Blood glucose 486, pH 7.28, HCO₃⁻ 14, ketones large, UOP 400 mL/hr, dry mucous membranes, poor skin turgor, K⁺ 3.2 |
| **Goal** | Patient will demonstrate improved hydration as evidenced by stable VS, urine output 30–50 mL/hr, and normalized blood glucose within 24 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. IV fluids (0.9% NS initially, then ½ NS when glucose < 250) 2. Regular insulin per protocol 3. Monitor glucose q1h 4. Monitor electrolytes q2–4h (especially K⁺) 5. Monitor ABGs 6. Neuro checks q2h |
| **Evaluation** | 8/11 2200: Glucose 248. pH 7.34. K⁺ 3.8 (repleted). UOP 40 mL/hr. Mucous membranes moist. Switched to D5½ NS + insulin. Goal partially met — continue protocol. |

### 7. Musculoskeletal: Total Hip Replacement

| Nursing Diagnosis | Risk for Falls related to impaired mobility and postoperative pain as evidenced by unsteady gait, use of assistive device, pain 6/10 with movement |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "I'm afraid of falling." Obj: POD #1 s/p total hip replacement, unsteady with walker, pain 6/10 with movement, follows hip precautions, incision C/D/I |
| **Goal** | Patient will remain free from falls during hospitalization |
| **Interventions** | 1. Bed alarm on 2. Call light within reach 3. Non-skid socks 4. Assist with all ambulation 5. Teach and reinforce hip precautions (no adduction past midline, no flexion > 90°, no internal rotation) 6. Pain management before PT |
| **Evaluation** | 8/13 1000: POD #3. Ambulated 150 ft with walker and 1 assist. No falls. Tolerated PT well. Discharge planning initiated. Goal met. |

### 8. Mental Health: Major Depressive Disorder

| Nursing Diagnosis | Risk for Self-Directed Violence related to depressed mood and feelings of hopelessness as evidenced by statements "I don't see the point," social withdrawal, flat affect, score 18 on PHQ-9 |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "Nothing matters anymore." Obj: Flat affect, poor eye contact, psychomotor retardation, withdrawn, PHQ-9 score 18 (moderately severe), denies suicidal ideation today |
| **Goal** | Patient will remain free from self-harm throughout hospitalization and verbalize one coping strategy by discharge |
| **Interventions** | 1. Q15 min checks (suicide precautions if indicated) 2. Remove sharp objects/belts 3. Therapeutic communication: active listening, nonjudgmental approach 4. Encourage participation in group therapy 5. Administer antidepressants as ordered 6. Involve family/support system |

### 9. OB: Postpartum Patient

| Nursing Diagnosis | Risk for Hemorrhage related to uterine atony as evidenced by boggy fundus, fundus at U+2, heavy lochia (saturated peripad in 30 min), BP 94/62, HR 112 |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: "I'm bleeding a lot." Obj: Fundus boggy, deviated right, U+2, peripad saturated x2 in 1 hour, lochia rubra large clots, BP 94/62, HR 112, RR 22 |
| **Goal** | Patient will maintain stable vital signs and fundal tone within 2 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. Massage fundus until firm 2. Have patient void (or catheterize) — full bladder displaces uterus 3. Oxytocin per orders 4. Monitor VS q15 min 5. Count pads q15 min 6. Maintain large-bore IV access 7. Type & crossmatch |
| **Evaluation** | 8/11 1530: Fundus firm at U. UOP 250 mL. Lochia moderate rubra. BP 108/70, HR 88. No clots. Goal met. |

### 10. Pediatric: Dehydration (Gastroenteritis)

| Nursing Diagnosis | Deficient Fluid Volume related to active fluid loss (vomiting/diarrhea) as evidenced by dry mucous membranes, sunken fontanelle (infant), decreased skin turgor, no tears when crying |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Assessment** | Subj: Parent reports "He hasn't kept anything down for 24 hours." Obj: 18-month-old, 6 wet diapers in 24 hours (baseline 8–10), dry mucous membranes, sunken fontanelle, skin turgor > 2 sec, no tears, irritable |
| **Goal** | Child will demonstrate adequate hydration as evidenced by moist mucous membranes, urine output 1–2 mL/kg/hr, and flat fontanelle within 24 hours |
| **Interventions** | 1. Oral rehydration solution (Pedialyte) small frequent amounts 2. IV fluids if unable to tolerate PO or severe dehydration 3. Strict I&O (including diaper weights) 4. Daily weight 5. Monitor electrolytes 6. Education on signs of dehydration |
| **Evaluation** | 8/12 0800: Tolerating Pedialyte. 4 wet diapers in 12 hours. Mucous membranes moist. Fontanelle flat. Weight increased 200g. Goal met. |

## Tips for Writing Better Care Plans

### Do's
- ✅ Use NANDA-approved diagnoses
- ✅ Include "related to" and "as evidenced by"
- ✅ Make goals SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- ✅ Include rationales for interventions
- ✅ Evaluate and revise as needed

### Don'ts
- ❌ Use medical diagnoses ("Pneumonia") instead of nursing diagnoses
- ❌ Write vague goals ("Patient will feel better")
- ❌ Copy interventions without rationales
- ❌ Forget to evaluate outcomes
- ❌ Use "Risk for" diagnoses with evidence (if evidence exists, use the actual diagnosis)

## How This Connects to NCLEX Clinical Judgment

The NGN case studies often present a scenario and ask you to:
1. **Recognize cues** — identify abnormal assessment findings
2. **Analyze cues** — determine what nursing diagnosis fits
3. **Prioritize** — which problem is most urgent?
4. **Generate solutions** — what interventions address the priority?
5. **Take action** — what's the FIRST action?
6. **Evaluate** — did the intervention work?
