# Content Gap Analysis — Obiomacare.com

**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Analyst:** OpenClaw
**Method:** Competitor content audit + keyword opportunity mapping

---

## Executive Summary

Obiomacare has a solid foundation with NGN-focused content, pharmacology mnemonics, and body system guides. However, several high-traffic nursing/NCLEX topic areas are either underdeveloped or missing entirely. This report identifies **12 priority content gaps** ranked by search opportunity and business value.

**Current Content Strengths:**
- NGN/Next Gen NCLEX deep dive (flagship)
- Pharmacology mnemonics (50+)
- Lab values reference
- Body systems guides (cardiac, GI, renal, etc.)
- Electrolyte mnemonics
- Free checklist lead magnet

**Missing Content Opportunities:**
- Interactive tools (calculators, care plan generators)
- Video content/transcripts
- Deep-dive study plans beyond 2 weeks
- Nursing school career content (beyond NCLEX)

---

## Priority 1: HIGH IMPACT — Missing Comprehensive Guides

### 1. Types of Shock (Detailed Comparison Guide)
**Why it matters:** 
- SimpleNursing, GoodNurse, and RegisteredNurseRN all rank for this
- High search volume: "types of shock nclex", "shock nursing", "hypovolemic vs cardiogenic shock"
- Natural NGN case study opportunity

**What to create:**
- Comprehensive guide: hypovolemic, cardiogenic, septic, neurogenic, anaphylactic, obstructive
- Comparison table (like GoodNurse's)
- NCLEX-style practice questions embedded
- Mnemonic for differentiation (e.g., "SHOCKED" from KOTC)
- Link to existing case studies

**Target keywords:**
- types of shock nursing
- nclex shock questions
- hypovolemic shock nursing interventions
- cardiogenic shock vs hypovolemic
- septic shock nclex

**Estimated difficulty:** Medium (competitors have strong content but opportunity exists)
**Business value:** HIGH — drives nursing students to case study product

---

### 2. EKG/Heart Rhythm Interpretation Guide
**Why it matters:**
- GoodNurse has "Heart Rhythm Strips Cheat Sheet"
- RegisteredNurseRN has extensive cardiac content
- Critical for NCLEX and clinical practice

**What to create:**
- Basic rhythm identification (sinus, afib, V-tach, V-fib, asystole)
- ECG changes for electrolyte imbalances (peaked T-waves in hyperkalemia)
- 12-lead EKG basics for nurses
- Mnemonics for arrhythmia treatment (e.g., "MONA" for MI)

**Target keywords:**
- ekg interpretation nursing
- heart rhythm strips nclex
- nclex cardiac arrhythmias
- ekg changes hyperkalemia

**Estimated difficulty:** Medium-High
**Business value:** HIGH — cardiac is a major NCLEX category
