## Content Template Requirements

Each article must include:
1. **Title tag**: Target keyword + "| Obioma Care"
2. **Meta description**: 150-160 chars with keyword
3. **H1**: Target keyword naturally phrased
4. **Table of contents**: Jump links for long content
5. **Downloadable PDF**: Lead magnet for email capture
6. **Related articles**: Internal links to other content
7. **CTA**: Link to paid products at bottom
8. **Schema markup**: Article + FAQ schema
