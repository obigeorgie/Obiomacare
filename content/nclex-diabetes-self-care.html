NCLEX Diabetes Self-Care: High-Yield Guide

The self-care teaching set for diabetes — what prevents heart attacks and strokes, foot care, sick-day rules, and blood sugar management.

1. Preventing Heart Attack and Stroke

Blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol levels are very important for preventing heart attacks and strokes — as is stopping smoking. Diabetes self-care is not just glucose control; it is cardiovascular risk reduction.

2. Foot Care

  • Daily foot inspection is part of diabetes self-care
  • Foot ulcers are a major diabetes complication to prevent and recognize early
  • Taking care of your feet is a core teaching topic

3. Sick-Day Care

Patients with diabetes need specific guidance for when they are sick, including when to check blood sugar more often and when to seek care.

4. Low Blood Sugar

Low blood sugar self-care is a distinct teaching topic — patients must know the signs and how to respond.

5. Tests and Checkups

Diabetes self-care includes regular diabetes tests and checkups — monitoring glucose, blood pressure, and cholesterol as part of the ongoing plan.
