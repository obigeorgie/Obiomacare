🚨 NCLEX Trap: Live vaccines (MMR, Varicella, FluMist) are CONTRAINDICATED in immunocompromised patients and pregnant women. If a child misses a dose, don't restart the series — just continue where left off. Rotavirus has strict age limits — cannot start after 15 weeks, must complete by 8 months.

4. Pediatric Calculations

📋 Essential Peds Calculations

💡 Weight Estimation (if scale unavailable):
1-12 months: (age in months + 9) / 2
1-5 years: (age in years × 2) + 8
6-12 years: (age in years × 3) + 5
CalculationFormulaExample
Fluid maintenanceFirst 10 kg: 100 mL/kg/day
Next 10 kg: 50 mL/kg/day
Remaining: 20 mL/kg/day		25 kg child: 1000 + 500 + 100 = 1600 mL/day
Hourly rateTotal mL / 241600 / 24 = 67 mL/hr
IV bolus (dehydration)20 mL/kg over 20 min (isotonic)25 kg: 500 mL NS/LR over 20 min
Medication dosingWeight-based (mg/kg)Always check max dose!
🚨 NCLEX Trap: Pediatric medication doses are almost always weight-based. ALWAYS double-check calculations. For fluid resuscitation in shock: 20 mL/kg bolus, can repeat. But in DKA, give 10-20 mL/kg NS over 1-2 hours (slower to prevent cerebral edema).
