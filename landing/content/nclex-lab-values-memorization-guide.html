1. Complete Blood Count (CBC)

The CBC is the most commonly ordered lab test. The NCLEX frequently tests your ability to identify abnormal values and connect them to patient conditions.

Neutrophils (50-70%) | Lymphocytes (20-40%) | Monocytes (2-8%) | Eosinophils (1-4%) | Basophils (0.5-1%)

🩺 CLINICAL PEARL: When you see low Hgb + low MCV + low Ferritin on the NCLEX, think iron deficiency anemia. The first intervention is iron supplementation (ferrous sulfate) with vitamin C.

2. Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP) / Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)

🧠 MNEMONIC: BUN/Creatinine Ratio
Normal ratio is 10:1 to 20:1
• >20:1 = Prerenal cause (dehydration, CHF, shock)
• <10:1 = Intrarenal cause (ATN, glomerulonephritis)
• Think: "Prerenal = Pumped up ratio"

🧠 MNEMONIC: "K⁺ MURDER" — Causes of Hyperkalemia
Medications (ACE-I, ARBs, K⁺-sparing diuretics)
Urine decreased (renal failure)
Rhabdomyolysis / burns / cell death
Diet (excessive K⁺ intake)
Endocrine (Addison's, acidosis)
Release from cells (hemolysis, transfusion)
💡 NCLEX TIP: If a question mentions "peaked T waves" on ECG, immediately think hyperkalemia. The first-line treatment is calcium gluconate to stabilize the myocardium, then insulin + glucose to shift K⁺ into cells.
🩺 CLINICAL PEARL: Always check magnesium when potassium is abnormal. Low Mg²⁺ makes hypokalemia refractory to treatment — you must replete Mg²⁺ first for K⁺ to respond.

4. Arterial Blood Gas (ABG)

ABG interpretation is a high-yield NCLEX topic. You need to identify acid-base disorders and determine compensation.

ParameterNormalInterpretation
pH7.35 – 7.45<7.35 = Acidosis | >7.45 = Alkalosis
PaCO₂35 – 45 mmHgRespiratory component. >45 = Respiratory acidosis | <35 = Respiratory alkalosis
HCO₃⁻22 – 26 mEq/LMetabolic component. <22 = Metabolic acidosis | >26 = Metabolic alkalosis
PaO₂80 – 100 mmHgOxygenation status. <80 = Hypoxemia
SaO₂95 – 100%Oxygen saturation. <90% = Severe hypoxemia
Base Excess-2 to +2 mEq/L<-2 = Metabolic acidosis | >+2 = Metabolic alkalosis
🧠 MNEMONIC: ROME
Respiratory Opposite — pH and PaCO₂ move in opposite directions
Metabolic Equal — pH and HCO₃⁻ move in the same direction
Example: pH 7.30 (↓) + PaCO₂ 50 (↑) = Respiratory acidosis (opposite directions)
🧠 MNEMONIC: Causes of Metabolic Acidosis — "MUDPILES"
Methanol | Uremia | DKA | Paraldehyde | Iron/INH | LLactic acidosis | Ethylene glycol | Salicylates
💡 NCLEX TIP: For ABG questions, always follow this 3-step method:
1. Look at pH — acidosis or alkalosis?
2. Look at PaCO₂ — respiratory component?
3. Look at HCO₃⁻ — metabolic component?
4. Is there compensation? (Partial = pH abnormal; Full = pH normal but both values abnormal)

• AST/ALT >2:1 = Alcoholic liver disease
• AST/ALT <1:1 = Viral hepatitis (ALT higher)
• Both >10× normal = Think acetaminophen toxicity, ischemic hepatitis, acute viral hepatitis

9. Thyroid Function Tests

TestNormalHyperthyroidismHypothyroidism
TSH0.4 – 4.0 mIU/LLow (<0.4)High (>4.0)
Free T40.8 – 1.8 ng/dLHighLow
🧠 MNEMONIC: TSH is the Boss
TSH and Free T4 always move in opposite directions in primary thyroid disease:
• High TSH + Low T4 = Primary hypothyroidism (Hashimoto's)
• Low TSH + High T4 = Primary hyperthyroidism (Graves')
If they move in the SAME direction, think pituitary/hypothalamic problem (secondary).

10. Proven Memorization Strategies for NCLEX Lab Values

Strategy 1: The Grouping Method

Don't memorize lab values in isolation. Group related values together:

  • Electrolyte cluster: Na⁺ (135-145), K⁺ (3.5-5.0), Ca²⁺ (8.5-10.5), Mg²⁺ (1.3-2.1) — all have decimals
  • CBC cluster: Hgb (12-18), Hct (37-52%) — Hct is roughly 3× Hgb
  • Kidney cluster: BUN (7-20), Creatinine (0.6-1.2) — ratio should be 10:1 to 20:1

Strategy 2: The Clinical Connection

Don't just memorize numbers — understand why they matter:

  • Platelets <50,000 = bleeding risk → implement bleeding precautions
  • K⁺ >6.0 = cardiac arrest risk → continuous ECG monitoring
  • INR >4.5 + bleeding = vitamin K administration

Strategy 3: The Mnemonic Arsenal

Create memorable phrases for groups:

  • WBC differential: "Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas"
  • Metabolic acidosis causes: "MUDPILES"
  • Hyperkalemia causes: "MURDER"
  • ABG interpretation: "ROME"
