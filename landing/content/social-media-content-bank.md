NCLEX tip: When you see a prioritization question, use ABCDE.

A — Airway (compromised = see first)
B — Breathing (O2 sat <90% = urgent)

D — Disability (neuro status)
E — Everything else (pain, comfort, education)

Pain is important. But a patient without an airway is dead.

### Twitter Post 12 (Thursday PM)
The 5 Rights of Delegation:

1. Right TASK (within scope)
2. Right CIRCUMSTANCE (patient is stable)
3. Right PERSON (has skills/training)
4. Right DIRECTION (clear instructions)
5. Right SUPERVISION (appropriate monitoring)

Miss one = error. Even if the task is simple.

### Twitter Post 13 (Friday AM)
Lab values you MUST know cold for the NCLEX:

• Potassium: 3.5-5.0 (critical: >6.0 or <2.5)
• Sodium: 135-145
• Glucose: 70-100 fasting
• Hgb: F 12-16, M 14-18
• Platelets: 150K-400K
• INR: 0.8-1.1 (2.0-3.0 on warfarin)
• Creatinine: F 0.5-1.1, M 0.6-1.2
• Troponin: <0.04

### Twitter Post 14 (Friday PM)
NCLEX pharmacology hack:

Don't memorize individual drugs. Memorize CLASSES.

ACE inhibitors → dry cough, hyperkalemia, angioedema
Beta blockers → bradycardia, masks hypoglycemia
Loop diuretics → hypokalemia, hypotension
Statins → myopathy, elevated LFTs
Insulin → hypoglycemia (#1 risk)

Know the class, know 90% of what you need.

Full guide: https://obiomacare.com/content/nclex-pharmacology-drug-classes.html
