🔤 The Big 3 Mnemonics

SALT LOSS — Causes of HypoNATREMIA

S · A · L · T · L · O · S · S
  • S — SIADH (syndrome of inappropriate ADH)
  • A — Addison's disease (adrenal insufficiency)
  • L — Loop diuretics (furosemide)
  • T — Thiazide diuretics
  • L — Loss of GI fluids (vomiting, diarrhea, NG suction)
  • O — Overhydration (psychogenic polydipsia, excess IV fluids)
  • S — Salt-wasting nephropathy
  • S — Syphilis / cerebral salt wasting (TBI, CVA)

MURDER — Signs of HYPERkalemia

M · U · R · D · E · R
  • M — Muscle weakness (legs → trunk → respiratory)
  • U — Urine changes (oliguria in renal failure)
  • R — Respiratory distress (diaphragm weakness)
  • D — Decreased cardiac contractility → bradycardia
  • E — ECG changes (peaked T waves → wide QRS → sine wave)
  • R — Reflexes decreased (flaccid paralysis)

CATS — Signs of HYPOcalcemia

C · A · T · S
  • C — Convulsions (seizures, tetany)
  • A — Arrhythmias (prolonged QT, bradycardia)
  • T — Tetany (Trousseau's, Chvostek's sign)
  • S — Spasms (laryngospasm, bronchospasm)

🩺 Causes at a Glance

Hypokalemia Causes

  • Diuretics (loop, thiazide)
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, NG suction
  • Insulin, beta-agonists
  • Hypomagnesemia (refractory)
  • Alkalosis (K⁺ shifts into cells)
  • Low K⁺ intake (alcoholism, anorexia)

Hyperkalemia Causes

  • Renal failure (#1)
  • ACE inhibitors, ARBs, K⁺-sparing diuretics
  • Cellular breakdown (crush injury, tumor lysis)
  • Acidosis (K⁺ shifts out of cells)
  • Excessive K⁺ replacement
  • Adrenal insufficiency

Hyponatremia Causes

  • SIADH (#1)
  • Heart failure, cirrhosis
  • Diuretics
  • Hypovolemia (3rd spacing)
  • Psychogenic polydipsia
  • Adrenal insufficiency

Hypernatremia Causes

  • Dehydration / water loss
  • Diabetes insipidus
  • Excess Na⁺ intake
  • Hypertonic saline
  • Cushing's syndrome
