🕐 Sepsis Hour-1 Bundle Checklist

Obioma | NCLEX Study Guide | obiomacare.com

⏰ TIME IS TISSUE — Complete within 1 HOUR

Suspected sepsis → qSOFA ≥2 OR SIRS + suspected infection → ACT IMMEDIATELY

📋 Step 1: Assess & Recognize (0-10 min)

Check qSOFA criteria
RR ≥22/min | Altered mentation | SBP ≤100 mmHg
qSOFA ≥2 = high risk of poor outcome → initiate bundle
Check SOFA score change ≥2
Respiratory (PaO₂/FiO₂), Coagulation (platelets), Liver (bilirubin), Cardiovascular (BP/vasopressors), CNS (GCS), Renal (creatinine/UOP)
Identify source of infection
Lungs, urine, abdomen, skin/soft tissue, bloodstream, CNS, device-related

🩸 Step 2: Obtain Cultures (Before antibiotics if possible)

Blood cultures: 2 sets from 2 different sites
Peripheral + peripheral, or peripheral + line (if line infection suspected). Volume: 10 mL per bottle. Clean site with chlorhexidine + alcohol.
Additional cultures per suspected source
Urine culture, sputum culture, wound culture, CSF if indicated. DO NOT delay antibiotics >45 min if difficult access.
⚠️ If obtaining cultures will delay antibiotics >45 minutes → give antibiotics FIRST, then cultures

💉 Step 3: Administer Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics (Within 1 hour)

Start empiric broad-spectrum antibiotics
Cover likely pathogens based on source + local resistance patterns. Typical: Vancomycin + Piperacillin-tazobactam OR Cefepime + Metronidazole.
Adjust for renal function, allergies, pregnancy
Check eGFR for dose adjustments. Verify allergy history. Consider pregnancy/lactation.
🔴 Every hour of antibiotic delay = 7.6% increase in mortality

💧 Step 4: Rapid Fluid Resuscitation (If hypotension or lactate ≥4)

Administer 30 mL/kg crystalloid IV bolus
Example: 70 kg patient = 2,100 mL. Use 0.9% NS or Lactated Ringer's. Complete within 3 hours.
Reassess perfusion after bolus
BP, HR, mentation, UOP, lactate. If still hypotensive → vasopressors (norepinephrine first-line).

🔬 Step 5: Measure & Monitor Lactate

Initial lactate measurement
Lactate ≥2 = tissue hypoperfusion (organ dysfunction). Lactate ≥4 = severe sepsis/shock criteria.
Recheck lactate within 2-4 hours
Goal: lactate clearance >10% or normalization. If not clearing → reassess fluid status, source control, antibiotic adequacy.
💡 NCLEX Memory Trick: "CLaM-B"
Cultures → Lactate → Meds (antibiotics) → Bolus (fluids)
Complete ALL within 1 HOUR of sepsis recognition
