Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Obioma Care, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What We Collect

  • Email address — when you download a free resource or make a purchase
  • Name — optional, to personalize emails
  • Purchase history — processed securely through Stripe
  • Usage data — page views via Google Analytics (anonymized)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver purchased products and free resources
  • To send educational emails (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our content and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted services to run Obioma Care:

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI compliant)
  • Hostinger — email delivery
  • Google Analytics — anonymized usage data
  • Firebase / Firestore — secure data storage

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies to understand traffic. You can disable analytics cookies in your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing admin@obiomacare.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations. Purchase records are kept for tax/accounting purposes.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@obiomacare.com.
