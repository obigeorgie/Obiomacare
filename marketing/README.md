## 🚀 Quick Start

### A. Social Posts (Postiz)
1. Open `social-posts/postiz-schedule.md`
2. Copy each post into Postiz
3. Schedule 1-2 posts/day for 7 days
4. Best times: 9 AM, 12 PM, 3 PM EST

### B. Email Campaign (Hostinger SMTP)
1. Open `email-campaigns/batch-6-announcement.html`
2. Import into your email tool (supports merge tags like `{{first_name}}`)
3. Send to your subscriber list
4. Track opens/clicks

**SMTP Settings:**
- Host: smtp.hostinger.com
- Port: 465 (SSL)
- User: admin@obiomacare.com
- From: admin@obiomacare.com

### C. Lead Magnet (PDF)
1. Open `lead-magnets/nclex-neuro-assessment-cheat-sheet.html`
2. Print to PDF (Chrome: Ctrl+P → Save as PDF)
3. Or host the HTML page and link to it
4. Gate behind email signup for lead capture

### D. Backlink Outreach (Hostinger Email)
1. Open `backlink-outreach/email-templates.md`
2. Personalize each email (name, school, specific post)
3. Use `backlink-outreach/prospects.csv` to track
4. Send 5 emails/day to avoid spam filters
5. Follow up on Day 7, 14, 30
