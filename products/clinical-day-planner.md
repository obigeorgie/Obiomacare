## QUICK REFERENCE

### Vital Signs: Normal Ranges
| Parameter | Adult Normal |
|-----------|-------------|
| BP | 90-120 / 60-80 |
| HR | 60-100 |
| RR | 12-20 |
| SpO2 | >95% |
| Temp | 97.8-99.1°F |

### When to Call MD
- SBP <90 or >180 (symptomatic)
- HR <50 or >120
- RR <10 or >30
- SpO2 <92% on O2
- Temp >101°F (post-op) or >100.4°F (neutropenic)
- Change in mental status
- Uncontrolled pain
- Any "something is wrong" feeling

### SBAR Reminder
- **S**: Situation — Who, what, where
- **B**: Background — Brief context
- **A**: Assessment — What you found
- **R**: Recommendation — What you need
