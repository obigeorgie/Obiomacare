# Clinical Judgment Mastery System — Product Content Outline

## Module 1: The NGN Decision Framework
**File**: `ngn-framework.pdf`
**Pages**: 15-20

### Contents:
1. **Clinical Judgment Model Overview**
   - Recognize Cues → Analyze → Prioritize → Take Action → Evaluate
   - How this maps to NGN case studies
   - The "experienced nurse" thinking pattern

2. **Recognize Cues: Signal vs. Noise**
   - Red flags that demand immediate action
   - Context-dependent interpretation (same number, different urgency)
   - Common cue categories: vital signs, lab values, patient behavior, history

3. **Analyze: The Questions I Ask Myself**
   - What's the underlying problem?
   - What could go wrong next?
   - What do I need to know that I don't know?

4. **Prioritize: Beyond ABC**
   - The ER nurse's prioritization matrix
   - When ABC isn't enough (multiple ABC issues)
   - Delegation rules and resource management

5. **Take Action: Intervention Hierarchy**
   - Least invasive first principle
   - When to call the doctor vs. handle it yourself
   - Documentation that protects your license

6. **Evaluate: The Forgotten Step**
   - Did the intervention work?
   - What changed? What didn't?
   - When to escalate
