Updated July 2026 • 8 min read

Khan Academy is the gold standard for free educational content. But if your child is struggling, falling behind, or needs a truly personalized path, there's a fundamental difference in how these two platforms approach learning.

The short answer: Khan Academy excels at delivering content. Obioma excels at knowing what content to deliver and in what order. If your child is stuck, Obioma identifies the exact prerequisite gap holding them back.

When Khan Academy Is Better

Khan Academy is unbeatable for:

  • Review and practice: If your child knows what they need to study, Khan's exercises are excellent.
  • Grade-level alignment: Following a standard curriculum? Khan maps perfectly to Common Core.
  • Free access: Zero cost for unlimited use — hard to beat for budget-conscious families.
  • Established subjects: Advanced math, test prep, and sciences have deep content libraries.

When Obioma Is Better

Obioma shines when:

  • Your child is stuck: Obioma diagnoses the exact prerequisite gap. Khan assumes you already know what to study.
  • You homeschool: No grade-level shackles. Obioma builds paths based on readiness, not age.
  • Learning gaps exist: A 10-year-old struggling with fractions might actually need place-value review from 2nd grade. Obioma catches this.
  • You need a plan: Obioma generates a complete step-by-step path with time estimates. Khan gives you a library, not a route.

Real-World Example

Emma is 9 years old and struggling with multiplication. Her parent signs her up for Khan Academy and assigns 3rd-grade multiplication exercises. Emma practices for weeks but still doesn't get it.

With Obioma, the parent runs a 5-minute assessment. The result: Emma never fully mastered skip-counting and repeated addition — prerequisites for multiplication. Obioma generates a 2-week path: skip-counting → repeated addition → arrays → multiplication. Emma is multiplying confidently in 10 days.

The difference: Khan Academy delivers the destination. Obioma builds the bridge to get there.

Can You Use Both?

Absolutely. Many Obioma parents use Khan Academy as the content delivery tool while using Obioma as the navigation system. Obioma tells you exactly which Khan Academy exercises to do — and in what order.

FAQ

Is Obioma a replacement for Khan Academy?

No — they're complementary. Obioma diagnoses and plans; Khan teaches. Use Obioma to identify what your child needs, then use Khan (or any resource) to teach it.

Why does Obioma cost money when Khan Academy is free?

Khan Academy is a nonprofit with massive funding. Obioma is a small, focused tool that builds personalized prerequisite graphs using AI. The free assessment is unlimited; the paid plan adds progress tracking, multiple learners, and the AI tutor.

Which is better for test prep?

Khan Academy, hands down. Their SAT/ACT prep is world-class. Obioma focuses on foundational K-5 skills and prerequisite chains.

Can Obioma work with my homeschool curriculum?

Yes. Obioma is curriculum-agnostic. Tell it any topic your child wants to learn, and it maps the prerequisites — regardless of what curriculum you're using.
