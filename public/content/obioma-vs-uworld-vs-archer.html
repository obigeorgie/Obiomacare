The NGN NCLEX Reality

Here's what most prep companies won't tell you: The NGN NCLEX changed in 2023.

It's no longer just "pick the right answer from 4 options." It's:

  • Case studies with multiple questions
  • Bow-tie and trend questions
  • Prioritization across multiple patients
  • Clinical judgment at every step

UWorld has great questions — but they don't teach you the thinking framework. They assume you already know how to clinically reason.

Archer has cheap questions — but their explanations can be thin, and some students report the questions feel easier than the actual NCLEX.

Obioma Care teaches the framework — the CJMM — so you can reason through ANY question, even ones you've never seen.
