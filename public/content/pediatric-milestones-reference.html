Pediatric Milestones Quick Reference

Motor, cognitive, language, and social milestones every nursing student must know for the NCLEX

By Nnamdi Okorafor, RN · Updated July 2026

Home › Pediatrics › Pediatric Milestones Quick Reference

(bottle) NCLEX Tip

Pediatric milestones are tested in "Select All That Apply" format. Know the KEY milestones for each age — the ones that are most distinct and clinically significant. Focus on the milestones that differentiate one age from the next.

0-3 Months The Reflex Period

Motor

  • Moro reflex present (birth-4mo)
  • Rooting reflex (birth-4mo)
  • Turns head side to side
  • Grasp reflex strong
  • Primitive stepping reflex

Cognitive/Social

  • Fixates on faces
  • Startles to loud sounds
  • Social smile at 6-8 weeks
  • Quiets to parent's voice

Language

  • Crying is primary communication
  • Cooing begins at 2-3 months
  • Different cries for different needs
4-6 Months The Discovery Period

Motor

  • Rolls front to back (4-5 mo)
  • Rolls back to front (5-6 mo)
  • Sits with support
  • Reaches for objects
  • Palmar grasp (uses whole hand)

Cognitive/Social

  • Stranger anxiety begins (6 mo)
  • Recognizes familiar faces
  • Laughs and squeals
  • Explores with mouth

Language

  • Babbling ("ba-ba", "da-da")
  • Turns to sounds
  • Makes raspberries
7-9 Months The Mobile Period

Motor

  • Sits without support (7-8 mo)
  • Crawls (7-9 mo)
  • Pulls to stand
  • Transfers objects hand to hand
  • Pincer grasp emerging (9 mo)

Cognitive/Social

  • Object permanence (9 mo)
  • Plays peek-a-boo
  • Looks for dropped toys
  • Prefers primary caregiver

Language

  • Understands "no"
  • Uses gestures (waving, pointing)
  • Imitates sounds
10-12 Months The Explorer Period

Motor

  • Pulls to stand, cruises (10-12 mo)
  • Walks with support (12 mo)
  • Stands alone momentarily
  • Pincer grasp refined (10 mo)
  • Throws objects

Cognitive/Social

  • Separation anxiety peaks
  • Imitates actions (phone to ear)
  • Explores environment actively
  • Shows preferences for toys

Language

  • First word (10-12 mo)
  • Follows one-step commands
  • "Mama" and "Dada" specific
1-2 Years The Toddler Period

Motor

  • Walks independently (12-15 mo)
  • Runs (18 mo)
  • Climbs stairs with help
  • Kicks ball
  • Scribbles with crayon

Cognitive/Social

  • Temper tantrums begin (18 mo)
  • Parallel play (plays next to, not with)
  • Shows independence ("no", "mine")
  • Object permanence fully developed

Language

  • 50+ words by 2 years
  • Two-word phrases ("more milk")
  • Points to named objects
  • Follows two-step commands
2-3 Years The Preschool Period

Motor

  • Jumps in place (2 years)
  • Walks up/down stairs alternating
  • Pedals tricycle (3 years)
  • Draws circle
  • Uses fork and spoon

Cognitive/Social

  • Parallel play → associative play
  • Imaginative play begins
  • Toilet training (2-3 years)
  • Knows own name and age

Language

  • 200+ words by 3 years
  • 3-word sentences
  • Asks "why?" constantly
  • Strangers understand 75% of speech
3-5 Years The School-Ready Period

Motor

  • Hops on one foot (4 years)
  • Skips (5 years)
  • Draws person with 6+ parts (4 years)
  • Dresses independently (4-5 years)

Cognitive/Social

  • Cooperative play (4-5 years)
  • Understands sharing and taking turns
  • Knows colors, counts to 10
  • Tells simple stories

Language

  • 1000+ words by 5 years
  • Complete sentences
  • Knows first and last name
  • Strangers understand 100% of speech

NCLEX Testing Strategy

Red flags = delayed milestones — these are the answer choices the NCLEX wants you to identify as abnormal:

  • Not sitting by 9 months
  • Not walking by 18 months
  • No words by 18 months
  • No 2-word phrases by 30 months
  • No stranger anxiety by 12 months (may indicate autism spectrum)
