🚨 NCLEX Emergency Drugs Quick Reference

ACLS meds, antidotes, and reversal agents

💔 Cardiac Arrest (ACLS)

Cardiac Arrest — VF/VT/Asystole/PEA

Epinephrine 1:10,000

1 mg IV/IO every 3-5 minutes

  • Vasopressor — increases coronary and cerebral perfusion pressure
  • Do NOT stop CPR to give
  • Follow each dose with 20 mL flush and elevate arm
Shockable Rhythm (VF/VT) — After 1st shock

Amiodarone

300 mg IV/IO bolus → 150 mg if persistent

  • Antiarrhythmic of choice for refractory VF/VT
  • Hypotension and bradycardia are common side effects
Torsades de Pointes

Magnesium Sulfate

1-2 g IV over 15 minutes

  • First-line for torsades (polymorphic VT with prolonged QT)
  • Also used for hypomagnesemia with arrhythmias

😤 Anaphylaxis

Anaphylaxis — First Line

Epinephrine 1:1,000

0.3-0.5 mg IM in lateral thigh (vastus lateralis)

  • Repeat every 5-15 minutes if symptoms persist
  • IM preferred over SC (faster absorption)
  • IV only in cardiac arrest setting

🩸 Opioid Overdose

Opioid Overdose — Respiratory Depression

Naloxone (Narcan)

0.4-2 mg IV/IM/IN → repeat every 2-3 min

  • Reverses respiratory depression within 1-2 minutes
  • Duration 30-90 min (shorter than most opioids) — monitor for re-sedation
  • May precipitate withdrawal in opioid-dependent patients

🍬 Hypoglycemia

Severe Hypoglycemia — Unconscious/Seizing

Glucagon

1 mg IM/SC (0.5 mg if <25 kg)

  • Raises blood glucose by stimulating glycogenolysis
  • Ineffective if glycogen stores depleted (starvation, chronic liver disease)
  • Nausea and hyperglycemia are common side effects
Conscious Hypoglycemia

Dextrose 50%

25 g (50 mL) IV push

  • Rapid correction of hypoglycemia
  • Vesicant — use large vein, monitor for infiltration
  • Follow with complex carbs to prevent rebound hypoglycemia

⚡ ACLS Bradycardia Algorithm

Bradycardia with Signs of Poor Perfusion

  1. Atropine 0.5 mg IV q3-5 min (max 3 mg)
  2. If atropine ineffective → Transcutaneous pacing
  3. If pacing delayed → Dopamine 5-20 mcg/kg/min or Epinephrine 2-10 mcg/min infusion
  4. Prepare for transvenous pacing if needed

Do NOT give atropine for Mobitz II or 3rd-degree heart block with wide QRS — pace immediately.

⚡ ACLS Tachycardia Algorithm

Unstable Tachycardia (Altered MS, Hypotension, Shock, Chest Pain)

  1. Synchronized cardioversion: 50-100 J (narrow regular), 120-200 J (narrow irregular), 100 J (wide regular)
  2. If cardioversion delayed → Adenosine 6 mg rapid IV push → 12 mg if no conversion
  3. For wide-complex tachycardia → Amiodarone 150 mg IV over 10 min
