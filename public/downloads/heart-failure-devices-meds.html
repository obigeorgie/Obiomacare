📊 NYHA Functional Classification

ClassSymptoms
INo limitation. Ordinary activity doesn't cause symptoms.
IISlight limitation. Comfortable at rest. Ordinary activity causes fatigue, dyspnea, palpitations.
IIIMarked limitation. Comfortable at rest. Less than ordinary activity causes symptoms.
IVUnable to carry out physical activity without symptoms. Symptoms at rest.

💊 Core HF Medications — "ABCDS"

ClassExamplesActionKey Monitoring
ACE-I/ARB/ARNILisinopril, Losartan, Sacubitril/valsartanReduce afterload, reverse remodelingBP, K⁺, Cr, cough (ACE-I), angioedema
Beta-blockerMetoprolol, CarvedilolReduce HR, myocardial O₂ demandHR, BP, signs of decompensation
CCB (if indicated)AmlodipineAfterload reductionPeripheral edema (not for HFrEF)
DiureticFurosemide, SpironolactoneReduce preload/fluid overloadK⁺, Cr, BP, daily weights
SGLT2-iDapagliflozin, EmpagliflozinReduce CV death & hospitalizationVolume status, UTI, ketoacidosis risk
⚠️ ACE-I + Spironolactone = Watch K⁺! Both raise potassium. Monitor closely. Add K⁺ supplement only if K⁺ <4.0.

🔋 Cardiac Devices

DevicePurposeKey Nursing Care
ICD (Implantable Cardioverter-Defibrillator)Prevent SCD from VT/VFCheck for shocks. Keep HOB <30° post-implant. MRI-conditional? Anxiety counseling.
PacemakerTreat bradyarrhythmias/heart blockMonitor for pacemaker syndrome. Keep HOB <30° post-implant. Check capture/sensing.
CRT (Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy)Biventricular pacing for dyssynchronyMonitor for improved EF/symptoms. Lead placement: RV, RA, coronary sinus (LV).
CRT-DCRT + ICD combinedAll of the above
LVAD (Left Ventricular Assist Device)Bridge to transplant or destination therapyINR 2-3 (warfarin). Monitor for thrombosis, infection, driveline care. No CPR (device damage). Assess for RV failure.
💡 NCLEX Memory Trick: "D-D-D" for Devices
Defibrillator (ICD) → shock VT/VF
Device for synchrony (CRT) → biventricular pacing
Device for pumping (LVAD) → mechanical circulation

⚡ ICD Shock — Patient Teaching

  • ✅ 1 shock: Rest, call provider within 24 hours
  • 🚨 ≥2 shocks or symptoms: Call 911 immediately
  • ⚠️ Drive? Restricted after shock until cleared (usually 6 months)
  • 📵 Electronics: Avoid strong magnetic fields (MRI, arc welding, induction stoves)
  • 📱 Cell phone: Keep >6 inches from device

🧠 Signs of Decompensated HF — "DOORS"

Dyspnea at rest or with minimal activity
Orthopnea (need pillows to sleep)
Oedema (peripheral, rapid weight gain >2-3 lb/day or 5 lb/week)
Rales/crackles (pulmonary congestion)
S3 gallop (volume overload)

📏 Daily Weights — Same scale, same time, same clothes
Gain >2-3 lb/day or >5 lb/week = fluid retention → call provider, may need diuretic adjustment.
