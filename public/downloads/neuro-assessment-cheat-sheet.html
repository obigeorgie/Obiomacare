Seizure First Aid

✅ DO

  • Note start time
  • Protect from injury
  • Turn on side after
  • Stay until awake
  • Document everything

❌ DO NOT

  • Put anything in mouth
  • Restrain patient
  • Give food/water
  • Leave alone

Status Epilepticus: >5 min or recurrent without recovery → EMERGENCY. First-line: Lorazepam 4 mg IV.

Increased ICP — Early vs Late Signs

EarlyLate (Cushing's Triad)
  • Headache (worse AM)
  • Nausea/vomiting
  • Altered LOC (earliest!)
  • Papilledema
  • ↑ Systolic BP
  • ↓ Heart rate (bradycardia)
  • Irregular respirations
  • → Brain herniation!
⚠️ NCLEX Trap: HOB 30–45°. Avoid neck/hip flexion. Hyperventilate ONLY for acute herniation (briefly) — not long-term.
