🧠 Stroke FAST Assessment + tPA Checklist

Obioma | NCLEX Study Guide | obiomacare.com

F

Face Drooping

Ask patient to smile. Is one side drooping or numb?

A

Arm Weakness

Ask patient to raise both arms. Does one drift down?

S

Speech Difficulty

Ask patient to repeat a phrase. Is speech slurred or strange?

T

Time to Call 911

Note exact time symptoms started. "Last known well" time.

🚨 EVERY MINUTE = 2 MILLION BRAIN CELLS LOST
Document exact "last known well" time. This determines treatment eligibility.

📋 Initial Assessment (First 10 Minutes)

Confirm symptom onset time — "Last known well"
If patient woke with symptoms, LKW = last time seen normal. Must be within 4.5 hours for tPA.
Check blood glucose
Hypoglycemia can mimic stroke. Treat if <60 mg/dL, reassess neuro status.
Complete NIH Stroke Scale
Score ranges 0-42. <4 = minor, 4-15 = moderate, 16-20 = moderate-severe, >20 = severe. Higher score = worse prognosis.
CT head without contrast (STAT)
Must rule out hemorrhage before tPA. Ischemic stroke = dark area. Hemorrhagic = bright (white) area.

✅ tPA (Alteplase) Inclusion Criteria

Age ≥18 years
Clinical diagnosis of ischemic stroke
Measurable deficit on NIHSS. Symptoms consistent with stroke.
Time from LKW ≤4.5 hours (3 hours standard, 4.5 extended)
Extended criteria: Age <80, NIHSS <25, no prior stroke + diabetes, not on anticoagulants.
CT negative for hemorrhage
BP <185/110 mmHg (treatable to goal)
Blood glucose >50 mg/dL

❌ tPA ABSOLUTE Contraindications

  • 🚫 Active internal bleeding or GI/GU bleeding within 21 days
  • 🚫 Intracranial hemorrhage history
  • 🚫 Recent intracranial/spinal surgery or serious head trauma (within 3 months)
  • 🚫 Ischemic stroke within 3 months
  • 🚫 Known intracranial AVM, aneurysm, or neoplasm
  • 🚫 Platelets <100,000, INR >1.7, aPTT elevated (if on heparin)
  • 🚫 SBP >220 or DBP >120 (refractory to treatment)
  • 🚫 Seizure at onset with postictal deficits (unless imaging confirms stroke)
  • 🚫 Symptoms rapidly improving or minor (NIHSS 0-1)
  • 🚫 Pregnancy
  • 🚫 Blood glucose <50 mg/dL (hypoglycemia must be corrected first)

💉 tPA Administration Protocol

Dose: 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg)
10% as IV bolus over 1 minute, then remaining 90% over 60 minutes via infusion pump.
NO anticoagulants or antiplatelets for 24 hours
Hold aspirin, heparin, warfarin. Repeat CT at 24 hours to rule out hemorrhage before restarting.
NO invasive procedures for 24 hours
No NG tubes, Foley catheters, arterial punctures, or IM injections.
Neuro checks: q15 min × 2 hr, q30 min × 6 hr, q1h × 16 hr
BP monitoring: q15 min during infusion, then per protocol
Goal BP <180/105. Treat with labetalol or nicardipine if exceeds.
🛑 STOP tPA if: Sudden severe headache, nausea/vomiting, acute hypertension, new neuro deficits, decreased LOC → STAT CT to assess for hemorrhagic conversion
💡 NCLEX Memory Trick: "tPA = Time, Platelets, Age, Blood Pressure"
Time <4.5 hours | Platelets >100K | Age ≥18 | BP <185/110

🩺 Post-tPA Care (First 24 Hours)

TimeframeAction
0-2 hoursNeuro checks q15 min, BP q15 min
2-8 hoursNeuro checks q30 min, BP q30 min
8-24 hoursNeuro checks q1h, BP q1h
24 hoursRepeat CT head. If no bleed → resume antiplatelet/anticoagulant per provider.
