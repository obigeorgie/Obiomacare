Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NCLEX Next Gen and clinical judgment.

What is the 3D Anatomy Lab?

Coming soon. We're building an interactive 3D exploration of 5 major body systems — cardiovascular, respiratory, nervous, digestive, and musculoskeletal. Click any organ to learn its structure, function, common diseases, and nursing implications.

How does the Spaced Repetition system work?

Coming soon. We're implementing the SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) algorithm, the gold standard for spaced repetition. After each quiz, you'll rate your confidence: Again (1 day), Hard (3 days), Good (default interval), or Easy (longer interval). The system schedules your next review at the optimal time to maximize retention.

What is the NCLEX Next Generation exam?

The NCLEX Next Generation (NGN) exam is the updated nursing licensure exam that tests clinical judgment using case studies, not just memorization. It uses the NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model to evaluate how nurses think, not just what they know.

How is Obioma different from UWorld?

While UWorld focuses on content review and traditional questions, Obioma trains you in the 6-step NCSBN framework. Our interactive NGN case studies match the exact format you'll see on exam day, with AI-powered interactive explanations.

Can international nurses use this platform?

Yes. We have specialized content for foreign-trained nurses including cultural context, US nursing norms, and visa guidance. Our community includes graduates from Nigeria, Philippines, India, and 20+ countries.

What is the Clinical Judgment Measurement Model?

The CJMM is the framework developed by NCSBN to measure clinical judgment. It has 6 cognitive skills: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, and evaluate outcomes.

How long should I prepare for NCLEX Next Gen?

Most students need 4-8 weeks of focused clinical judgment training. We recommend 1-2 hours daily of case study practice plus weekly assessment reviews. Our adaptive system targets your weak areas.

What are SATA questions and how do I beat them?

Select All That Apply (SATA) questions are the most feared on NCLEX. The secret: treat each option as a separate true/false question. Don't look for patterns — evaluate each option independently. When in doubt, pick the safest choice.
