Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Obioma Care, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address — when you sign up for our free NCLEX Study Checklist or email newsletter
  • Name — optional, when provided in forms
  • Payment information — processed securely through Stripe; we do not store credit card details
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, quiz scores (anonymized for product improvement)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver purchased products and free resources
  • Send educational content and NCLEX tips (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improve our content and user experience
  • Process payments securely
  • Comply with legal obligations

Email Communications

When you download our free checklist or enter your email, you join our email list. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email. We use Resend for email delivery.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI compliant)
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Google Analytics — anonymized usage statistics

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. All payment processing is handled by Stripe's secure infrastructure. We never store credit card numbers on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails anytime
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, email us at admin@obiomacare.com.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at admin@obiomacare.com.
