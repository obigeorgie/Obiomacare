# NCLEX Trap of the Day — Video Series

## Overview
Short-form vertical videos (9:16, 30-45s) designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
Each episode covers ONE high-yield NCLEX trap with clinical judgment framing.

## Format
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — Bold statement with real stakes
2. **Scenario (3-18s)** — Clinical situation with decision point
3. **Answer (18-35s)** — Correct action + reasoning
4. **Payoff (35-42s)** — Saveable rule/mnemonic
5. **CTA (42-45s)** — Follow + website
